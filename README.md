# Al Tabib Al Hafid
![logo](./assets/logo_colors/logo_tabib.png)

Althabib Alhafid – Medical Students' Club Portal

An online verification website for the Althabib Alhafid Medical Students' Club of faculty of medical sciences algeirs - Algeria, allowing students to check their admission status and access their assigned course groups.

# Key Features
- Flexible Student Search: Order-agnostic search supporting special characters, accents, and varied name orderings.
- Instant Status Lookup: Quickly verify whether a student is accepted or unlisted.
- Instructor & Group Routing: Displays assigned professors alongside direct join links to their corresponding Telegram study groups for accepted students.
